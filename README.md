## 🎯 The Problem & Our Mission

During course registration weeks at Istanbul Technical University (ITU), thousands of students simultaneously attempt to secure limited spots in mandatory engineering core classes, faculty pools, and popular electives. Desired sections often fill up in mere seconds.

### Common Frustrations Faced by Students:
- **Manual F5 Fatigue:** Students spend endless hours manually refreshing the ITU OBS page hoping a single seat drops.
- **Sleepless Nights & Missed Drops:** When someone drops a class in the middle of the night or early morning, it is almost impossible to catch it without automated assistance.
- **The Major Restriction Trap:** Even when a seat opens up, students often rush to register only to find out the seat is reserved for another department or class year.
- **Newly Added Sections:** Academic departments frequently open extra sections (new CRNs) mid-registration. Students who aren't constantly watching the catalog miss out before sections reach capacity.

**itüdis** was created to eliminate this stress. Operating continuously in the cloud, it autonomously checks ITU course schedules around the clock and sends real-time email alerts the exact instant an eligible seat opens or a new section is created.

## 🧠 Smart Major & Program Restriction Filter

At ITU, many high-demand courses partition their seats across different departments. For example, in a physics section:
- 10 seats may be reserved for Computer Engineering (BIL),
- 10 seats for Mechanical Engineering (MAK),
- 5 seats for Electrical Engineering (ELK).

If an engineering student drops a class, the total available seats may increase, but if that seat isn't allocated to your program, the university portal will reject your registration attempt.

**How itüdis Protects Your Time:**  
By saving your primary department (or double major program), itüdis checks the reservation breakdown and only notifies you when the open seat matches your program. No false alarms, no wasted energy.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Do I need to keep the website open or leave my computer turned on?
**No.** Monitoring runs continuously in the cloud. You can close your browser, turn off your device, or go about your day. Alerts will arrive in your email inbox regardless of your device state.

#### How frequently are course quotas checked?
Quotas are checked automatically on a recurring schedule designed to detect openings promptly without causing undue load on university infrastructure.

#### What should I do if every single section of a required class is full?
Use the **Course Code Tracking** option (for example, `BLG 223E`). This tracks all current sections at once and immediately notifies you if faculty administrators open an additional section (new CRN).

#### Does itüdis automatically register for the course on my behalf?
**No.** To comply with academic honor codes and security standards, itüdis does not automate the actual registration action. It acts as an early warning radar so that you can open the official ITU portal and claim the seat yourself.
